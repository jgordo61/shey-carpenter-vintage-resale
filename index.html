Shey Carpenter is a New York and Los Angeles-based model and certified personal stylist. With a background in fashion and a decade of experience dressing real people for real life, she is known for her eye for vintage proportion, modern silhouette, and building wardrobes that feel authentic — not trend-driven. Her vintage shop offers a carefully curated edit of one-of-a-kind pieces sourced from estate sales, markets, and private collections. One-on-one styling sessions are available virtually and in-person in New York and Los Angeles.
